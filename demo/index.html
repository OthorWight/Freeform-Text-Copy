Try Freeform Text Copy

Copy just the text inside a rectangle you draw.

  1. Open Chromium’s puzzle-piece menu and click Freeform Text Copy. You can pin it for quicker access.
  2. Drag a rectangle over some sample text below, then release to copy it.
  3. Click the paste box and press Ctrl + V (or ⌘ + V on Mac).

Click the extension again for each selection. Hold Alt while dragging to preserve spacing. Press Esc to cancel.
